About

Babel Boost

I'm an independent author and digital designer creating practical guides for busy readers. From Iceland travel guides, to quick cookbooks, health and longevity guides, mindfulness, and everyday skills — every book has the same mission: clear structure, thoughtful design, and tools you can use right away. Expect concise guides, workbooks, and creative planners built around checklists and simple, practical steps.
